Before you sign up

Free never means risk-free.

A few minutes of care protects your work, your people, and your wallet.

01

Check the current deal

Read the pricing page and sign-up screen before you depend on a quota. Look for trial-only language, card requirements, automatic renewals, watermarks, and feature gates.

How “last verified” works →
02

Protect private information

Never paste patient details, client records, passwords, API keys, identity documents, or unpublished work you cannot afford to expose. Local-first tools reduce cloud exposure, but they are not automatically risk-free.

The privacy checklist →
03

Know what you can use

Digital output is not automatically yours to publish or sell. Plan restrictions, model licenses, public galleries, and copyright rules all matter. For health topics, use these tools for education only, never diagnosis or treatment.
